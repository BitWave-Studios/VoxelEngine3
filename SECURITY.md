# VoxelEngine3 - Security Guide

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you encounter a security issue or a potential vulnerability, do not open a public
> issue or pull request.
> 
> You are expected to follow the security policy outlined below.
> Any questions can be sent to our ModMail bot via our [Discord].

The full policy, along with a full disclosure process and expected response times
can be found on our Org page, [BitWave Studios] or on our [website]
